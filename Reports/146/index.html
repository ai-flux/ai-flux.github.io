Yingzhi XBOT Launches Universal Food-Service Robot Lineup with "One Brain, Many Forms" Embodied Intelligence Architecture

Reports Jul 3, 2026

Leiphone (WeChat official account: Leiphone) reported that on June 30, Yingzhi XBOT held its "2026 XBOT Universal Food-Service Embodied Robot Launch" in Beijing. Built on four pillars — products, platform, commercial closed loop, and ecosystem openness — the company unveiled its proprietary embodied OS XOS 3.0, the AI Agent "AiBao Store Manager," and three new embodied robots spanning coffee, ice cream, and full-scenario service. The three collectively deliver a "One Brain, Many Forms" intelligence architecture.

XOS 3.0: The Embodied OS Purpose-Built for "One Brain, Many Forms"

XOS 3.0 serves as the technical foundation beneath AiBao Store Manager — the de facto "operating system" of embodied intelligence. Its architecture enables cross-embodiment skill transfer: a capability learned once remains reusable after a form-factor change, delivering "develop once, deploy across a thousand forms." This突破了 long-standing industry barriers of high migration costs and weak generalization.

Three core capabilities underpin XOS 3.0: (1) a VLA (Vision-Language-Action) architecture, conferring end-to-end "see-understand-execute" ability; (2) a full-chain R&D closed loop spanning algorithms, hardware, interaction, and productization, accelerating lab-to-commercial deployment; (3) a data flywheel — real-world deployments generate multimodal operational data that fuels system iteration, producing a virtuous cycle: more deployments yield richer scenarios, which yield stronger capabilities.

To date, Yingzhi XBOT has deployed more than 1,000 devices across 100+ cities globally, producing over 4 million cups of specialty coffee and logging over 4 million human-machine interactions.

AiBao Store Manager: The Embodied Intelligence That Steps In Front of the Customer

On the XOS 3.0 foundation, Yingzhi XBOT has formally launched AiBao Store Manager as a standalone AI Agent brand.

XOS 3.0 is the learning and evolution engine; AiBao Store Manager is the interface that brings that engine to the customer — it has a face, a voice, and management capability. Unlike conventional unattended machines that "work but do not speak," AiBao Store Manager fuses front-end user interaction with back-end operations. Front-end: it recognizes faces and voices, proactively engages, recalls preferences, and executes the full dialogue-to-service pipeline with human-like fluency. Back-end: it acts as the store's operational brain, monitoring inventory, equipment health, and business metrics in real time, shifting the paradigm from "humans chasing problems" to "problems surfacing themselves." The result: every device is staffed by an indefatigable, continuously learning "store manager."

XOS 3.0's Three "Body Forms" Reshape Food-Service Embodied Hardware Standards

Powered by the XOS 3.0 brain and AiBao Store Manager's unified service persona, Yingzhi XBOT unveiled three hardware products — the same intelligence architecture rendered across distinct physical forms:

XBOT C3 Coffee Robot (¥219,000, JD.com debut in July)

Positioned as "master-grade extraction + AI-driven personalization." Powered by XOS 3.0 and the AiBao Store Manager interface, it delivers full-process intelligence from voice ordering and preference recognition to precision beverage preparation. Throughput: 80 cups/hour, 45 seconds per cup, 150-cup tank续航. Positioning repeatability: ±0.02mm. AI latte art is supported — each cup functions as an "emotion business card." Design life exceeds 5 years; core components have passed million-cycle fatigue testing.

XBOT i3 Ice Cream Robot (¥179,000, JD.com debut in July)

The i3 was born from the C3's beverage capabilities via XOS 3.0's cross-embodiment transfer and generalization, running the same intelligence core and AiBao Store Manager interface. It supports free combination of 4 sauces and 4 crunchy toppings, with throughput ≥60 cups/hour and a design life of 250,000 cups. The i3 itself is direct product-market evidence of the "develop once, reuse across a thousand forms" principle.

XBOT X1 Universal Food-Service Humanoid Robot (expected late 2026)

The X1 is the technological flagship: 7-axis dual arms, 3-4kg end payload, 500 TOPS on-device compute, and stable offline operation. It runs full XOS 3.0 and AiBao Store Manager, covering the end-to-end loop — "cup pickup → making → placement → delivery → completion" — and has already acquired skills such as cocktail shaking and noodle boiling. A standout feature: the robot's purpose-designed "work uniform," which functions as both labor protection and a food-safety "professional certification," clearing regulatory and trust hurdles for mass commercial deployment.

XBOT COFFEE and XBOT Lab: Proving a New Commercial Model for Embodied Intelligence

Technology has a foundation; products form a matrix. But embodied intelligence commercialization must answer one question: does this model actually generate profit in real business settings?

At the launch, Yingzhi XBOT officially launched its proprietary coffee brand XBOT COFFEE and released operational data from its flagship experience store, XBOT Lab — validating the technology-to-commerce closed loop with genuine store performance.

XBOT Lab is a human-machine collaboration store — robots and human staff operate in concert, each with distinct responsibilities. A six-axis dual-arm collaborative robot handles consistent output and store operations: extraction, milk frothing, and latte art at industrial-grade precision, plus beverage management and operational planning — pushing standardized, repetitive work to "ten thousand cups, one standard." This is XOS 3.0's "One Brain, Many Forms" in a live retail setting. Human staff focus on packaging, restocking, and maintenance, channeling more effort into customer engagement and emotional connection.

XBOT COFFEE is branded as "AI Trendy Coffee," built on the equation "Great Taste × Fun = Coffee with Character." Quality is non-negotiable: specialty beans and premium milk are standard. XOS 3.0's precision control enables full quantification of extraction volume, water-to-powder ratio, and caffeine content.

Strategic Partnerships with JD.com and Others to Tap the Trillion-Yuan Embodied Intelligence Market

Yingzhi XBOT and JD Group signed a strategic cooperation agreement at the event. JD.com has long served as the primary launch platform for Yingzhi XBOT's hardware robots — from last year's XBOT Coffee Robot Lite and first-generation ice cream robot, to today's XBOT C3, XBOT i3, and the year-end X1 humanoid.

Rooted in多年的合作 and a shared conviction about AI-driven new consumption, the two sides will target deep collaboration across餐饮零售, chain coffee, and smart-store scenarios. Drawing on JD.com's strategic resources in the intelligent robotics sector, the partnership spans product R&D, marketing, channel build-out, and technology enablement. Early exploratory work — anchored on use cases such as "JD Qixian Coffee" — has begun, pushing embodied intelligence to demonstrate and validate commercial value in real, high-frequency consumer settings.

Looking globally, as Yingzhi XBOT's overseas footprint expands, both parties will explore further collaboration in cross-border logistics and technical operations, drawing on JD.com's global supply chain and service network to underpin Yingzhi XBOT's international expansion.

Beyond JD.com, Yingzhi XBOT has finalized strategic ecosystem agreements with multiple ODM/OEM partners backed by solution brands — including Yizhuang Robot, Fic Robot, Jilin Tourism Holding Group, Bowu Tianxing, and US-based BLUESKY.
