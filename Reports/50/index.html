🚀 The Ten Most-Starred GitHub Open-Source Projects of May 2026 🔥

Reports 2026-06-28 04:05:46

Welcome to the May 2026 edition of the GitHub Open-Source Project Rankings. From a pool of 17 monthly candidates, ten projects have been selected for their outstanding long-term tracking value, spanning engineering skill repositories, code graphs and persistent memory, multi-agent orchestration, industry-specific plugin blueprints, AI engineering curricula, and fully autonomous short-video generation. Collectively, they signal a decisive shift within the open-source ecosystem: agents are evolving from isolated single-session interactions into reusable, collaborative, and deliverable production assets.

Presented below are the ten most compelling projects that have earned a star in May 2026.

---

skills

🌟 Star Count: 113.3K+ (monthly increase of 65,737)

🧰 Skills for Real Engineers: A library of engineering skills curated from production-grade .claude directories

skills is an engineering skill repository open-sourced by Matt Pocock from his production .claude directory. It encompasses code review, test-driven development, refactoring, pull requests, and release processes — authentic engineering workflows codified as reusable assets. By transforming team defaults into version-controlled, forkable, and iterable Skill packages, it has amassed over 65,000 stars in a single month, making it the highest-velocity project of May and a definitive benchmark for the skills-as-assets paradigm.

Engineering defaults: review, testing, and release pipelines are natively mountable into Claude Code. Standardized Skill structure: self-contained directories paired with a SKILL.md manifest, conforming to the Agent Skills open specification. Team-forkable architecture: adaptable across language stacks and branching strategies via straightforward rewriting. Market-leading monthly velocity: the data confirms that encoding workflows as Skills has transitioned from an experimental practice to an operational imperative.

💡 skills is recommended for frontend and full-stack engineering organizations, as well as technical leaders seeking to elevate Claude Code from an individual productivity aid to a team-scale process asset.

👉 Explore now: GitHub

codegraph

🌟 Star Count: 35.7K+ (monthly increase of 34,446)

🕸️ Pre-Indexed Code Knowledge Graph: Reduces File Reads and Tool Invocations for Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor

codegraph pre-indexes codebases into queryable local knowledge graphs, thereby eliminating the need for coding agents to iteratively grep or read in order to locate entry points and call chains. According to official benchmarks, this approach yields substantial reductions in token consumption and tool-invocation volume. The platform supports Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, OpenCode, Hermes Agent, and additional stacks, and is architected for 100% local execution — source data never exits the host machine.

Local pre-indexing: the knowledge graph functions as the agent's primary retrieval layer, supplanting ad-hoc file scanning. Token and tool-call reduction: the efficiency gains are most pronounced in large-monorepo environments. Multi-agent compatibility: a single index serves heterogeneous terminal-based coding agents. Notable monthly velocity: the 34,000-star increase confirms that 'agent-cost anxiety' has emerged as a pervasive operational concern.

💡 codegraph is strongly indicated for organizations managing large monorepositories, extended coding sessions, and excessive tool-invocation patterns that degrade performance and inflate costs.

👉 Try it now: GitHub

---

Understand-Anything

🌟 Star Count: 47.5K+ (monthly increase of 37,390)

🗺️ Transforming Code into Explorable Knowledge Graphs: Graphs That Instruct Outweigh Graphs That Impress

Understand-Anything scans arbitrary code repositories and transforms them into interactive, searchable, and queryable knowledge graphs augmented with structured walkthroughs. It facilitates rapid comprehension of unfamiliar codebases, seamless team handoffs, and the generation of architectural maps that remain intelligible to external stakeholders. In contrast to codegraph — which targets retrieval-cost optimization — Understand-Anything is purpose-built for comprehension, pedagogy, and onboarding.

Interactive knowledge graph: fully explorable and queryable — an antithesis to static documentation. Understanding-first philosophy: prioritizes genuine comprehension over visual spectacle. Multi-toolchain compatibility: operable across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI, and other environments. Monthly growth of 37,000 stars: a velocity that surpasses several higher-ranked projects, underscoring acute demand for code-comprehension tools.

💡 Understand-Anything is well-suited for developers and technical leads inheriting legacy codebases, navigating large-scale software systems, and managing organizational knowledge transfers.

👉 Explore now: GitHub

---

ruflo

🌟 Star Count: 57.0K+ (monthly increase of 23,191)

🌊 A Multi-Agent Orchestration Platform for the Claude Ecosystem: Enabling Swarm-Based Collaboration, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and Native Claude Code Integration

ruflo (originally developed under the RuFlo direction) is an agent-orchestration platform introduced by ruvnet for the Claude ecosystem. It supports the deployment of multi-agent swarms, coordination of autonomous workflows, integration of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and native alignment with Claude Code and Codex. The platform marks a decisive architectural progression from single-agent CLI paradigms toward a dedicated orchestration tier. Although it does not appear among the top 10 by ranking position, its monthly growth of 23,000 stars and cumulative total of 57,000 stars indicate substantial engineering-validation from the community.

Multi-agent swarm orchestration: enables parallel task decomposition and self-learning collective intelligence at scale. Enterprise-grade architecture: a unified fabric spanning workflows, retrieval-augmented generation, and conversational systems. Native Claude Code and Codex integration: directly serves the most active cohort of coding-agent users. Ascendant orchestration tier: complements the skills and codegraph ecosystems, forming a 'capability-context-collaboration' triad.

💡 ruflo is well-suited for organizations seeking to scale agent deployments from personal scripts to team-level collaboration, leveraging dashboard-grade orchestration capabilities and parallel multi-agent execution.

👉 Try it now: GitHub

agentmemory

🌟 Star Count: 20.3K+ (monthly increase of 18,071)

🧠 Persistent Memory for Coding Agents: Retaining Project Conventions and Historical Conclusions Across Sessions

agentmemory addresses the cross-session forgetting problem inherent to coding agents by delivering a framework-agnostic persistent memory layer. This layer enables agents to retain project conventions, configuration preferences, historical decisions, and learned patterns over extended periods, thereby minimizing the need to re-establish context with each new session. The repository emphasizes real-world benchmark performance and has been architected as a drop-in memory plugin for existing Claude Code and Codex workflows.

Cross-session reuse: project conventions and prior conclusions are persistently accumulated across sessions. Framework-agnostic design: integrates with heterogeneous agent stacks without requiring architectural rewrites. Optimized for long-cycle projects: delivers material value in multi-module, multi-repository collaboration environments. Complementary to codegraph: the former governs 'repository structure' while the latter governs 'collaborative memory.'

💡 agentmemory is recommended for teams operating on extended project cycles where context retention — such as 'how a prior modification was implemented' — is critical to productivity.

👉 Install now: GitHub

financial-services

🌟 Star Count: 29.1K+ (monthly increase of 21,308)

🏦 Anthropic's Financial Services Plugin Blueprint: Packaging Role-Based Capabilities into Reusable Assets

financial-services is a suite of financial-industry plugins open-sourced by Anthropic. It demonstrates how research, compliance, reporting, and related financial workflows can be organized into skills, commands, and toolkits that are natively loadable by Claude Cowork and Claude Code. Its primary value proposition is that of an official reference architecture for 'industry plugin construction,' rather than a single-purpose script demonstration.

Industry-standard templating: provides an organizational blueprint for financial-domain workflows and modular capability components. Role-based plugin architecture: directly extensible to proprietary industry-plugin implementations. Anthropic provenance: embodies the prevailing paradigm for Claude ecosystem pluginization. Enterprise (ToB) reference: purpose-built for organizations requiring version-controlled standard operating procedures.

💡 financial-services is recommended for financial-services organizations, enterprise (ToB) product divisions, and architects engaged in the design of Claude plugin ecosystems.

👉 Explore now: GitHub

academic-research-skills

🌟 Star Count: 25.2K+ (monthly increase of 21,119)

📚 Academic Research Skills for Claude Code: Research → Write → Review → Revise → Finalize

academic-research-skills decomposes the academic paper lifecycle into mountable Skill chains — retrieval, composition, peer review, revision, and finalization — and is designed for extended knowledge-generation tasks including research papers, literature surveys, and grant proposals. The project exemplifies the vertical-Skills trend observed in this month's ranking: transforming methodology into repeatable, versionable assets.

End-to-end skill chain: encompasses every stage of the academic research pipeline. Reusable Skill architecture: obviates the need to author mega-prompts de novo for each project. Long-horizon task suitability: optimized for structured annotation, iterative peer review, and multi-round revision workflows. Vertical-Skills exemplar: the academic orientation complements skills' engineering focus, establishing a 'general-purpose plus domain-specific' dichotomy.

💡 academic-research-skills is recommended for academic researchers, graduate students, and content-production teams requiring a rigorous research workflow.

👉 Read now: GitHub

ai-engineering-from-scratch

🌟 Star Count: 26.0K+ (monthly increase of 19,640)

🧱 Building an AI Engineering System from Scratch: Learn It. Build It. Ship It for Others.

ai-engineering-from-scratch is a structured AI engineering curriculum designed to guide learners from foundational concepts to production-ready deliverables — explicitly resisting the notebook-demo ceiling. It serves as a counterweight to this month's conspicuous 'Agent tool' narrative: regardless of the volume of available tools, engineering rigor remains a prerequisite for system delivery.

Structured curriculum: a complete closed loop spanning learning, implementation, and delivery. Build-and-Ship orientation: architected for deployable, maintainable production systems. Agent-stack complement: addresses the engineering competencies that extend beyond 'Claude Code proficiency.' Team onboarding reference: well-suited as a baseline for internal AI engineering training programs.

💡 ai-engineering-from-scratch is recommended for developers seeking to systematically acquire AI engineering competencies and progress from API-consumption workflows to full product construction.

MoneyPrinterTurbo

🌟 Star Count: 74.8K+ (monthly increase of 16,993)

🎬 One-Click High-Definition Short Video Generation Powered by Large Language Models: Automated Scripting, Voiceover, Subtitling, and Footage Assembly

MoneyPrinterTurbo is a mature AI-driven short-video automation platform: upon receiving a topic input, it orchestrates the full pipeline — script generation, voiceover synthesis, subtitle rendering, footage retrieval, and video assembly — making it well-suited for rapid content-idea validation and batch production of short-form videos. With a cumulative star count approaching 75,000, the project occupies the 11th position on the monthly chart; nevertheless, its sustained practical utility and community validation substantially exceed those of several smaller projects appearing higher in the ranking.

End-to-end pipeline automation: a complete workflow spanning topic selection through final video rendering. Multi-model compatibility: interoperable with diverse large-language-model and text-to-speech backends. Substantial star equity: a content-production tool validated through prolonged community adoption. In alignment with broader agent trends: 'content automation' persists as an independent growth trajectory.

💡 MoneyPrinterTurbo is recommended for short-video content creators, social-media operations teams, and AIGC practitioners requiring rapid iteration on content hypotheses.

Pixelle-Video

🚀 AI Fully Automated Short Video Engine: Fully Automated Short Video Engine

Pixelle-Video is a fully autonomous short-video engine developed by AIDC-AI, distinguished by its emphasis on a more production-grade, engineering-oriented video creation pipeline. Conclusion

The ten projects selected from the 17 May 2026 candidates can be organized along four principal thematic threads:

Skills and plugin assetization: skills, academic-research-skills, and financial-services codify engineering actions, research methodologies, and industry-specific roles into reusable Skills and plugin assets. Context engineering: codegraph, Understand-Anything, and agentmemory address agent-cost reduction across the three dimensions of retrieval efficiency, code comprehension and knowledge transfer, and cross-session memory persistence. Orchestration and collaboration: ruflo elevates multi-agent orchestration to a first-class concern within the Claude ecosystem, bridging the final gap between individual CLI-based operation and team-scale collaborative workflows. Content automation and engineering rigor: MoneyPrinterTurbo and Pixelle-Video exemplify AIGC-driven content automation, while ai-engineering-from-scratch reinforces the thesis that engineering discipline remains indispensable beyond tool proficiency.

Projects that did not qualify for this month's Top 10 yet merit continued attention include: andrej-karpathy-skills (profiled in the April edition), RuView (profiled in March), ViMax, UI-TARS-desktop, and others. CloakBrowser, 9router, and analogous entries were excluded from this edition's recommendations due to compliance and terms-of-service concerns.

May 2026 has proven to be another month worthy of a star.
