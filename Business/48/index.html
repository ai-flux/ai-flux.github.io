Liang Wenfeng Returns to the Spotlight — A Force to Be Reckoned With.

Business 2026-06-28 04:05:46

Liang Wenfeng Makes Headlines Once More.

The annual National College Entrance Examination, commonly referred to as the Gaokao, has only recently drawn to a close.

As circumstance would have it, multiple references have surfaced on Xiaohongshu in recent days recounting the Gaokao narrative of a leading figure within China's domestic artificial intelligence ecosystem.

Indeed, this prominent figure is Liang Wenfeng, the founder of DeepSeek.

The formidable capabilities of DeepSeek are now widely recognized across the industry.

What remains far less appreciated, however, is that Liang Wenfeng had already established an unparalleled track record during his own Gaokao administration more than two decades ago.

More than two decades ago, Liang Wenfeng distinguished himself as the top-performing Gaokao candidate in his locality, having achieved an exceptional score of 806 points.

This achievement was widely covered by media outlets and print publications at the time, and remains verifiable through documented records.

The predominant public perception of Liang Wenfeng is one of deliberate understatement and reserve.

Despite having iterated through numerous model releases across various scales, DeepSeek has conspicuously abstained from any product launch events or marketing campaigns.

Liang Wenfeng himself maintains an extremely low public profile — so much so that online image searches yield only a handful of recurring photographs, which has sustained widespread curiosity about his personal and professional journey.

Accordingly, this article undertakes a systematic examination of Liang Wenfeng's academic and entrepreneurial trajectory, tracing the sequential progression by which he ascended to become a preeminent entrepreneur within the AI industry.

The Academic and Entrepreneurial Trajectory of Liang Wenfeng

Public records indicate that Liang Wenfeng was born in 1985 in Zhanjiang, Guangdong Province, into a household of educators, with both parents serving as primary school instructors.

Having exhibited exceptional academic aptitude since childhood, Liang Wenfeng distinguished himself scholastically, with a particular proficiency in mathematics.

Upon completion of his primary education, Liang Wenfeng secured direct matriculation to Wuchuan No.1 High School through subject-based academic competitions, where he subsequently completed both his junior and senior secondary schooling.

During his junior secondary years, Liang Wenfeng's overall academic performance was not yet considered particularly distinguished, as he displayed an uneven subject profile — though his aptitude in mathematics and physics was notably exceptional.

Upon entering senior secondary school, however, Liang Wenfeng's academic performance underwent a qualitative transformation. This trajectory accelerated markedly following his enrollment in the school's Olympiad preparatory program, and by his final year, he consistently maintained a position within the top three of his cohort.

In 2002, Liang Wenfeng secured admission to the undergraduate program in Electronic Information Engineering at Zhejiang University on the strength of his exceptional Gaokao performance, thereby commencing his tertiary education.

To what extent did his Gaokao performance stand out? As previously noted, his score of 806 points secured him the designation of top-ranked examinee in his locality.

Although his Gaokao score had surpassed the admission threshold of Tsinghua University, he ultimately elected to enroll in the Electronic Information Engineering program at Zhejiang University, as he considered the curriculum and research orientation there to be more closely aligned with his academic interests.

Following the completion of his undergraduate degree, Liang Wenfeng pursued further academic training at Zhejiang University, securing admission in 2007 to the master's program in Information and Communication Engineering, thereby commencing his graduate research career.

During his graduate tenure at Zhejiang University, Liang Wenfeng conducted research under the supervision of Professor Xiang Zhiyu, with a specialized focus on computer vision, and was conferred his master's degree in 2010.

Throughout his undergraduate and graduate tenure at Zhejiang University, Liang Wenfeng cultivated a profound interest in financial markets, which — when combined with his outstanding background in mathematics and the natural sciences — effectively laid the foundation for his subsequent entrepreneurial pursuits.

Furthermore, numerous like-minded acquaintances cultivated by Liang Wenfeng during his time at Zhejiang University subsequently evolved into key collaborators along his entrepreneurial trajectory.

Notwithstanding his strong academic credentials, Liang Wenfeng elected not to pursue a conventional career trajectory. Rather, having cultivated a pronounced interest in financial markets during his university years, he astutely identified the substantial potential inherent in quantitative investing through the application of mathematics and artificial intelligence.

In 2008, amid the throes of the global financial crisis, Liang Wenfeng formed a dedicated team and commenced exploration into the feasibility of fully automated quantitative trading.

In 2013, Liang Wenfeng, together with his Zhejiang University peers, co-established Hangzhou Jacobi Investment Management Co., Ltd., an enterprise dedicated to investment management and advisory services. The firm's nomenclature reportedly draws inspiration from the German mathematician Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi, serving as a tribute to their profound reverence for the discipline of mathematics.

Two years thereafter, in 2015, Liang Wenfeng, in conjunction with his founding team, co-established High-Flyer Quant, a quantitative investment firm built upon the application of mathematics and artificial intelligence.

At that juncture, the founding team operated with notable vigor and unwavering diligence, having set their sights on establishing a preeminent quantitative hedge fund of global standing.

Remarkably, Liang Wenfeng was merely 30 years of age at the time.

High-Flyer Quant is widely recognized as a prominent institution within the quantitative investment landscape, having at its peak overseen assets under management approaching the hundred-billion-yuan threshold and establishing itself as a leading force in the quantitative market.

Concurrently, High-Flyer Quant has consistently maintained its focus on the development and deployment of quantitative investment strategies that are fundamentally anchored in mathematics and artificial intelligence.

The year 2016 marked a pivotal inflection point for High-Flyer, during which the firm formally completed the AI-driven transformation of its quantitative strategies. The inaugural equity positions generated by deep learning algorithmic models were deployed for live trading, powered by GPU-accelerated computation. Concurrently, the enterprise experienced accelerated growth over the subsequent years, accompanied by a rapid expansion in assets under management.

By 2021, High-Flyer's assets under management had breached the hundred-billion-yuan threshold, securing its position among the upper echelon of China's quantitative private fund sector. Simultaneously, Liang Wenfeng had firmly established his reputation within the industry as the understated 'Quantitative Trading Monarch.'

At that juncture, Liang Wenfeng had reached precisely 36 years of age.

Following his extraordinary success in the quantitative investment arena, Liang Wenfeng redirected his focus toward the domain of artificial general intelligence — a sector widely regarded as possessing immense growth potential — thereby reaffirming his capacity for prescient strategic vision and setting the stage for the DeepSeek narrative.

In 2023, Liang Wenfeng formally incorporated DeepSeek, marking his official entry into the Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) sector.

DeepSeek is an innovative technology enterprise dedicated to the exploration of cutting-edge AGI research and the implementation of real-world applications.

Furthermore, DeepSeek's objectives extend beyond the development of high-performance large language models, encompassing a commitment to investigating the fundamental nature of AGI while advancing the broader ecosystem.

As articulated in the company's corporate philosophy:

> Commit to the pursuit of understanding AGI's fundamental nature; reject mediocrity; approach challenges with intellectual curiosity, and address the most consequential questions through the lens of the longest possible time horizon.

As Liang Wenfeng noted during a prior interview, given that large language models represent an indispensable pathway toward AGI and have already demonstrated nascent AGI-like characteristics, DeepSeek elected to initiate its efforts from this strategic starting point.

Subsequent to its founding, despite maintaining a relatively compact research and development team, DeepSeek achieved substantial breakthroughs within a remarkably short span of just over one year, rolling out multiple models in succession.

The unprecedented fervor that accompanied the release of DeepSeek-R1 remains vividly etched in collective memory. That milestone represented a watershed moment for China's domestic AI sector, sending reverberations throughout Silicon Valley.

From that pivotal juncture onward, the oligopolistic stranglehold previously held by a handful of international technology conglomerates over the large AI model landscape was definitively dismantled. Through its commitment to open-source architecture, operational efficiency, and cost-effective technological innovation, DeepSeek meaningfully accelerated the commoditization and widespread accessibility of large AI models.

This, ultimately, constitutes the paramount significance of DeepSeek's arrival on the global stage.
