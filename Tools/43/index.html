DingTalk's "Wukong" Secures the World's First AI Management System International Certification, Marking a New Standardized Phase for AI Governance

Tools 2026-06-29 03:28:25

As artificial intelligence permeates enterprise management, ensuring AI application security and compliance has become an industry imperative. DingTalk's enterprise-grade AI agent platform "Wukong" has recently achieved a milestone in this arena — securing the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI management system certification.

Notably, this standard — the world's first AI management system framework — was jointly published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC), conferring exceptional authority and benchmark status.

During the certification process, the "Wukong" platform satisfied the rigorous demands of international standards across AI governance frameworks, security compliance systems, and full lifecycle management. DingTalk had initially secured this certification in 2025; the subsequent accreditation of "Wukong" signals the company's successful extension of mature AI governance capabilities from infrastructure to enterprise-grade agent applications.

As generative AI proliferates across productivity tools, the security foundation of digital transformation grows ever more critical. By consistently pursuing international AI management system certification, DingTalk has demonstrated its frontier exploration in technology while offering enterprises a replicable paradigm for building transparent, controllable, and secure intelligent work environments.
