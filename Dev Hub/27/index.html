23rd ChinaJoy Set for July 31: "Travel with AI" to Reshape Global Digital Entertainment Landscape

Dev Hub Jun 29, 2026

The 23rd China International Digital Entertainment Expo & Conference (ChinaJoy) kicked off its 2026 press conference at the Shanghai International Convention Center today, where organizers confirmed a July 31–August 3 window at the Shanghai New International Expo Center. Taglined "Travel with AI," the event is positioned around an unmatched game-trial roster and a frontier AI-tech ecosystem, designed to present the full breadth of the digital entertainment sector's latest advances.

Scale hits new high: more than 1,000 game titles and trending products confirmed for exhibition.
